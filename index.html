Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ALTLY.

How does the free trial work?

Our 14-day free trial gives you full access to all features of the Professional plan. No credit card is required to start. At the end of the trial period, you can choose to continue with any of our paid plans or cancel with no obligation.

Can I integrate with my existing CRM?

Yes! ALTLY integrates seamlessly with most major real estate CRMs including Salesforce, Follow Up Boss, LionDesk, and more. Our implementation team will help you set up the integration during onboarding.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. ALTLY is month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime with just a few clicks. We're confident you'll love our platform, but we don't believe in locking you in.

How secure is my client data?

Client data security is our top priority. We use bank-level encryption (AES-256) for all data at rest and in transit. We're SOC 2 Type II compliant and undergo regular third-party security audits. Your data is always yours and is never sold or shared.

What kind of support is included?

All plans include email support with a 24-hour response time (Mon-Fri). Professional and Enterprise plans include priority phone support during business hours. We also offer comprehensive onboarding and training for all new customers.
